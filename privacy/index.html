Privacy Policy

Effective date: 8 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how FilmCampaignOS processes information when an authorized user connects supported social-platform integrations.

1. Information we may process

  • Account and profile information made available by an authorized social-platform integration.
  • Public social content and associated metadata made available through the authorized integration.
  • Authentication and authorization information required to maintain an authorized connection.
  • Technical information necessary to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and audit the service.

2. TikTok integration

When a user authorizes the TikTok integration, FilmCampaignOS may access the TikTok data permitted by the scopes granted to the application. For the current TikTok integration, this includes basic user information and public videos. FilmCampaignOS does not request or use TikTok publishing permissions for this read-only integration.

3. How information is used

Information is used to provide social listening and analytics features, normalize and analyze authorized social data, maintain connection health, support auditability, and protect the service.

4. Sharing

FilmCampaignOS does not sell personal information. Information may be processed by service providers or infrastructure required to operate the application, subject to appropriate safeguards and applicable agreements.

5. Retention and deletion

Information is retained only as needed for the authorized service, operational, security, legal, and audit purposes applicable to the deployment. Users may revoke a third-party authorization through the relevant platform and may request deletion of applicable application data.

6. Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards designed to protect information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

7. Third-party services

Third-party platforms have their own privacy policies and terms. Users should review the policies of any platform they connect to FilmCampaignOS.
