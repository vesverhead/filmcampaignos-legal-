FilmCampaignOS

FilmCampaignOS is a social listening and analytics workspace for authorized film campaign teams to monitor and analyze social content.

Third-party integrations

FilmCampaignOS may connect to third-party social platforms when an authorized user explicitly grants access. The application is designed to request only the permissions required for its enabled integrations.
